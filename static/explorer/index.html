What do subjects and themes mean here?

Subjects are semantic topics inferred by a pinned sentence transformer, with explicit abstention when uncertain. Each subject has one governed theme in a versioned catalog. Event families and named entity mentions remain separate. Model assignments are provisional until independently reviewed. Similarity scores are not probabilities. This view stops at themes.
